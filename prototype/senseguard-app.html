Adherence check — confirming your scheduled dose.

Pick up any strip or bottle and point the rear camera at it. SenseGuard checks identity, expiry and schedule on-device, then tells you what to do.

What's real here: camera capture, motion-based stability gate, device orientation/heading, live GPS-accuracy location confidence, barcode detection, voice + haptic output, and the verification/decision logic are all running for real in your browser right now.

Simulated for this prototype: the OCR/vision "identify" step — you confirm the medicine from a short list instead of true text recognition (the sandboxed hosting here can't run an OCR model; the production Android build uses on-device OCR/vision per the problem statement). Planned next: clinician voice capture → structured instruction card.
